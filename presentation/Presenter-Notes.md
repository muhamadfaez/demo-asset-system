# IIUM KAMS — client presentation

**Video:** `IIUM-KAMS-Tutorial-English-Voiced.mp4`

**Length:** 2 minutes 34 seconds
**Format:** 1920 × 1080, English captions and synthetic English voiceover, subtle sound effects and background tones

The video uses actual system screens with demonstration data. Animated highlights guide viewers through administrator and regular-user workflows.

## Before presenting

1. Download or copy the MP4 to the presentation laptop.
2. Open it in a video player and test full-screen playback before the meeting.
3. The video plays offline. Its captions are already visible; no subtitle setup is required. Check speaker volume before presenting.
4. If using PowerPoint, insert the MP4 as a video from your device.

## Suggested introduction

“This is IIUM KAMS, an asset management workspace for administrators and staff. In the next two and a half minutes, I’ll show how assets are registered, found, requested, maintained, and reported.”

“Which workflow would you like to try first with your own team: asset registration, borrowing, or maintenance?”
